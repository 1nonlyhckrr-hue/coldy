# Voice leaderboard

Reference: https://docs.zynrax.site/configuration/leaderboard . Original Emika bounded adaptation,not full reference parity. Native Discord acceptance pending.

`/voicelb enable`,`disable`:fresh Manage Server authority,voice events must be enabled to start. Off by default. Enabling/disable replaces policy revision and drops existing samples. Already connected members receive no startup/enable backfill;future observed joins only. `config` shows saved switch and your privacy preference. `stats` shows only your settled milliseconds for current UTCday,today+previous6UTCdays,and retained90UTCdays. `top period page`:private10IDs/counts perpage,stable total-descending/ID-ascending order. Parameter periods are not extra paths. `resetmine` erases your counts/active sample;`optout` also excludes future samples;`optin` allows future joins without existing-call backfill. Private3locale shared Emika embeds/no-ping/JSONexports.

Once per60seconds,force-fetch native human membership,voice state and normal GuildVoice channel;exactguild/user/channel identities,nonbot and nonAFK required. Stage channels excluded. Policy revision/privacy/current session must still match after reads. Credit only elapsed intervals up to120seconds with native reads taking no more10seconds. Split precisely at UTCday boundaries. Long gaps/failed native lookups drop uncertain intervals;restart loses in-memory sessions,keeps settled counts. Move/disconnect tails never credited. Mutes/deaf state do not affect eligibility:these are sampled connected-time counts,not time speaking or quality. Gateway misses between two successful samples can still overstate connected time;this is not a certified continuous-presence measurement. Privacy/disable/reset races and overlapping samples are guarded. Maximum100active samples/guild and1000global;excess joins untracked until a futurejoin fits. Every60seconds each active sample needs3native reads;rate limits can drop intervals and stop that session. No audio/voicecontent/channelID history persisted;only guild/user/day counts and policy/privacy stored.

90UTCday retention,not all-time tracking. No manually invented voice credit,daily rolling24hour window,memberstats/rewardroles/inviteattribution. No startupbackfill,automatic role/member effects,announcements or DMs. Sampling and privacy behavior require nativegateway/rate-limit/client verification before live use.
